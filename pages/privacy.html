Privacy Policy

Clear disclosure of how this front-end website handles interface preferences, enquiry forms, and uploaded resume previews.

Privacy & Data Handling Summary

Editable Privacy Template: Update this policy with your agency's official privacy officer details, retention schedules, and applicable regional privacy disclosures before connecting a live backend.

1. Front-End Form & Resume Handling

In its current front-end demonstration state, HireBridge does not transmit, collect, or store personal data entered into the Candidate Enquiry form, Employer Enquiry form, Login/Register forms, or Newsletter input. Selected resume files are checked locally in your browser for file format (.pdf, .doc, .docx) and size only and are never uploaded to a server.

2. LocalStorage Browser Preferences

To preserve your viewing preferences across page loads, the site stores two non-identifying UI keys in browser localStorage: hb_theme (light or dark) and hb_dir (ltr or rtl). No tracking cookies or third-party advertising profiles are set by our scripts.

3. Future Backend Integration

When connected to an authorised recruitment backend, candidate CVs, contact details, and employer hiring briefs should be processed solely for recruitment coordination, candidate evaluation, and requested consultations in compliance with applicable data protection standards.

4. External Assets

Typography is loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), while site photography is served directly from the local images/ directory.

5. Privacy Enquiries & Requests

Once live contact channels are configured on our Contact page, individuals may request access, correction, or deletion of submitted recruitment records via the designated agency contact address.
