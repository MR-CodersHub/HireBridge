01 · Permanent Hiring

Permanent Recruitment

Our permanent recruitment offering supports employers building lasting teams. From initial role definition to candidate onboarding coordination, every phase follows a transparent structure.

  • Role Requirement Understanding: Detailed consultation covering responsibilities, team context, and core criteria.
  • Candidate Sourcing & Initial Screening: Focused identification and assessment of suitable professionals.
  • Shortlisting & Interview Coordination: Curated shortlists, coordinated interviews, and hiring process support through offer stage.
