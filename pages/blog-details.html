Understanding Every Stage After Submission

Applying is only the first step. Most structured recruitment journeys move through profile screening, an introductory conversation, role-specific interviews, and a final alignment check before an offer is discussed.

Knowing how each stage works helps you prepare the right evidence, respond promptly to consultant updates, and stay confident while hiring decisions are made.
