Permanent Recruitment

Structured hiring support designed to connect employers with qualified, long-term professionals aligned to team culture and operational goals.

Service Overview

Purpose & Operational Focus

Permanent recruitment focuses on identifying professionals ready to contribute over the long term. Rather than forwarding high volumes of unvetted applications, HireBridge begins by aligning closely on responsibilities, expectations, and team context.

Every candidate stage—from initial brief to final offer coordination—is managed with structured communication so employers make confident hiring decisions and candidates remain clearly informed.

Key Benefits

How This Service Supports Your Goals

Structured advantages built for clarity, alignment, and organized hiring coordination.

Aligned Role Briefing

Thorough definition of skills, experience, and workplace expectations before sourcing starts.

Focused Candidate Shortlists

Pre-screened profiles selected for genuine alignment rather than unfocused CV volume.

Structured Screening

Consistent assessment covering capability, motivation, and role suitability.

Coordinated Scheduling

Organized interview logistics saving internal teams hours of administrative back-and-forth.

Honest Communication

How the Service Works

Six-Stage Delivery Process

Every engagement follows six clear phases from initial consultation through post-interview follow-up.

1

Initial Discussion

Connect with our team to outline objectives, scope, and priorities.

2

Requirement Assessment

Clarify role criteria, timing, employment structure, and suitability markers.

3

Sourcing or Opportunity Identification

Identify aligned candidates or roles matching the verified requirements.

4

Screening & Coordination

Evaluate suitability, prepare clear notes, and coordinate next steps.

5

Interview or Application Support

Facilitate scheduling, briefing context, and timely two-way feedback.

6

Follow-Up

Assist with offer coordination and post-start check-ins to support transition.
